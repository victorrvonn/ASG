# ASG

## Registration:
1) It will register users with Google OAuth allowing people from our workspace only.
2) There would be two types of accounts: Learning Volunteers and Teaching Volunteers.
3) For registration of Learning Volunteers, the following data will be taken: Name, Roll No., Batch, Phone No., Languages known, preferred language
4) For registration of Teaching Volunteers, the following data will be taken: Name, Roll No., Batch, Subjects, Phone No., Languages comfortable with
5) Learning Volunteers account will be created directly. Teaching Volunteer account requests will be sent to admin for verification.
6) A person (single email ID) can have both types of accounts. Give an option for people to switch account type in their profile section (Or register for other type if haven't)


## Features:
1) The home page will have a stream that would display all polls, forms, materials, doubts based on most recent.
2) The menu will display Account, Doubts, Sessions, Polls/Forms, Materials, About ASG and Feedback

### Features for Learning Volunteers:
Everything visible to a Learning Volunteer should be specific to their Batch.
1) Doubts: When they open Doubts, they can see the recent doubts asked by people. They will have a search bar  There will be a button to "Ask a doubt", clicking which they can choose a subject, ask a question, can add pictures and post. They can choose to post a doubt anonymously. Doubts will open as conversation threads where anyone (both Learning and Teaching volunteers) can converse and attach images as well. There would be a filter option of subjects.
2) Sessions: They would see the announcements of classroom sessions and their own requests (not others'), and on top will get options of "Request Classroom Session", "Request Group Session", "Request one-on-one session". When they request for any session, the form should input Subject, Specific topic expected to cover, Preferred Time, Preferred Language(s)
3) Polls/Forms: They would see all the active polls and forms posted by Teaching Volunteers, and they can respond to them.
4) Materials: They can access all the materials posted by Teaching Volunteers, with a filter option of subjects.
5) Feedback: This would be Paragraph Type textbox where people can post feedback anonymously. One Learning Volunteer can send multiple feedbacks, the textbox will reset once "Send" is hit.

### Features for Teaching Volunteers:
1) Doubts: They can see all the doubts posted, filtered according to subjects and batch. When a teaching volunteer posts something in the conversation thread of a doubt, Their nameplate would explicitly mention along with name, Teaching Volunteer and Batch. Teaching Volunteer would see a option of "Notify People" in a doubt thread, clicking which the learning volunteers respective batch should receive notification regarding the doubt and the link to that thread.
2) Sessions: They can see all the active session requests by the learning volunteers, with a filter option of subjects and batch. Session requests can be closed by the Teaching Volunteers.
3) Polls/Forms: They can see all the active polls and forms. They can access the responses of the forms. They can post Polls and Forms, dedicated to a specific batch, where they can add questions of their choice (Types: Short Answer, Paragraph Answer, Single Select, Multiple Select, Upload Files) and also choose if they want responder's identity or want them to answer anonymously. Polls and Forms can be closed by Teaching Volunteers or they can also add a time after which it will get closed automatically.
4) Materials: They can post materials for a subject, specific to a batch. They can add text or files.
5) Feedback: They would be able to view all the feedbacks submitted.


## Notifications:
The notifications should be shared in Whatsapp. There would be a community consisting: Batch-wise Learning Volunteers Announcements & Teaching Volunteers Announcements. In all the groups, only the bot will have the permission to send messages. Whenever someone registers as a Learning Volunteer, the link to their respective group should be showed to them in the confirmation page. Also add the Group Links in the Accounts page.

### Notifications for Learning Volunteers (Batch specific)
1) Whenever a classroom session is announced.
2) Whenever a poll/form has been posted, link to that should be shared.
3) Whenever a material is posted, link to that should be shared.
4) Whenever a teaching volunteer chooses to notify people about a doubt.

### Notifications for Teaching Volunteers
1) Whenever a person requests to be a teaching volunteer, link to that request should be shared.
2) Whenever a doubt is posted.
3) Every 24 hours, maybe everyday 12 am, post a single message having all the active session requests.
4) Every 24 hours, maybe everyday 12 am, post a single message having the current result of all active polls.
5) Whenever a feedback is posted.
